How Registration Works

Follow these 4 simple steps to complete your schedule

  1. 1

    Explore Catalog

    Search courses by department, keyword, schedule, or prerequisites using our comprehensive search and filtering tools.

  2. 2

    Check Eligibility

    Verify course prerequisites and seat availability (Open, Waitlist, or Full) before initiating your registration.

  3. 3

    Submit Registration

    Select your grading preference (Letter Grade, P/NP, Audit), review policy statements, and confirm your enrollment.

  4. 4

    Manage Schedule

    View your enrolled course table in your Student Profile, monitor total units, or drop classes if your plans change.
