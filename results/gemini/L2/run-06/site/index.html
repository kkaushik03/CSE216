How Registration Works

Follow these 4 simple steps to ensure a smooth course registration experience.

  1. 1

    Explore & Filter Catalog

    Browse courses by subject, delivery mode, or instructor. Verify prerequisites before adding.

  2. 2

    Select Your Term Schedule

    Choose classes that balance your weekly load (12 to 18 credits max for full-time standing).

  3. 3

    Confirm in Accessible Modal

    Review course meeting times, locations, and credits in the confirmation modal before saving.

  4. 4

    Review Registered Schedule

    Check your enrolled courses data table, export or print your schedule, and check degree progress.
