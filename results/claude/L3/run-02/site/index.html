How registration works

  1. Explore

    Search and filter the catalog by department, level, credits, and time of day.

  2. Choose

    Pick a course and term. We check for full sections, time conflicts, and credit limits.

  3. Confirm

    Review your details in the confirmation dialog, then submit.

  4. Manage

    See every registered course in your profile and drop courses if plans change.
