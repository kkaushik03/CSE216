How registration works

  • 1. Find courses

    Search the course catalog and filter by department, level, day and open seats.

  • 2. Fill in the form

    Choose up to 18 credits on the registration page. We check time conflicts for you.

  • 3. Confirm

    Review your choices in a summary, then confirm. Your seats are saved straight away.

  • 4. Manage your schedule

    See and drop registered courses, and update your details, on your profile.
