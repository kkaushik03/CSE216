How registration works

  1. Find courses

    Search the course catalog and filter by department, level, credits or open seats.

  2. Add them to your plan

    Select “Add to plan” on each course card. Your plan is saved on this device.

  3. Confirm your registration

    On the registration page, check your details and confirm. See your courses on your student profile.
