How registration works

  1. 1

    Find courses

    Search and filter the catalog by department, level, credits and meeting days.

  2. 2

    Add to your list

    Select the courses you want. We’ll keep them ready on the registration page.

  3. 3

    Confirm registration

    Complete the form, review your choices and confirm. We’ll check for time conflicts.

  4. 4

    Manage your schedule

    See all registered courses on your profile and drop a course if your plans change.
